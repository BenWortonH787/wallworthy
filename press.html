Wallworthy

Press kit

Wallworthy is an iPhone app for keeping children's artwork. Scan the whole backpack pile at once, frame each drawing, keep it with your child's voice telling the story, and share one gallery with the family.

Fast facts

  • Name: Wallworthy: Kids Art Gallery
  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 18 or later
  • Price: Free. Wallworthy Plus is a one-time $9.99 in-app purchase. No subscription.
  • Privacy: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, nothing collected. Art stays on the phone and in the family's own iCloud.
  • Made by: Ben, an independent developer and parent
  • App Store: apps.apple.com/app/id6829036669

What it does

  • Scan the whole backpack at once. Each drawing in a stack is found, straightened and cleaned on the phone, then you tap which child made it.
  • A gallery for each child. Every piece is framed with a museum-style label: title, artist, age and date, grouped by school year.
  • Their voice. The child records what the drawing is, and the recording stays with the art.
  • Shared family gallery (new in 1.1). Invite a partner or grandparents. Everyone sees the same drawings and voice notes, and anyone can add more. Free, using Apple's iCloud sharing, with no Wallworthy server.
  • Send to family as a picture, or as a short video with the child's voice.
  • Wallworthy Plus ($9.99 once): Bring to life (the drawing dances), Art Show videos, a printable 8 × 8 inch Year in Art picture book, extra frames and a widget.
