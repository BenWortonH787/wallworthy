Help

How do I scan a whole stack at once?

Tap + and keep scanning pages one after another. When you're done, pick drawings and tap the child who made them. Repeat until every piece has an artist, so siblings' art can come out of one scan.

What's included in Wallworthy Plus?

Bring to life, Art Show videos, Year in Art picture books, four extra frames and the home-screen widget. It's a single payment, not a subscription. Scanning, voice notes, iCloud backup and export are always free.

I bought Plus but it's locked on my new phone.

Open the ••• menu → Wallworthy Plus → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. With Family Sharing, family members can restore it too.

How does backup work?

If iCloud is on for Wallworthy (Settings → [your name] → iCloud), your galleries are backed up to your private iCloud automatically and appear on your other devices. You can also use Export everything to save a copy that opens on any computer.

How do I leave kid mode?

Tap the 🔒 Kid mode button at the top of the screen and answer the grown-up question.

How do I print a book?

Open a school year and choose Make the picture book. You can print it with AirPrint, or save the 8 × 8 inch PDF and upload it to any photo-book service.
