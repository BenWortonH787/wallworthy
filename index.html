Help

How do I scan a whole stack at once?

Tap + and keep scanning pages one after another. When you're done, pick drawings and tap the child who made them. Repeat until every piece has an artist, so siblings' art can come out of one scan.

What's included in Wallworthy Plus?

Bring to life, Art Show videos, Year in Art picture books, four extra frames and the home-screen widget. It's a single payment, not a subscription. Scanning, voice notes, iCloud backup and export are always free.

I bought Plus but it's locked on my new phone.

Open the ••• menu → Wallworthy Plus → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. With Family Sharing, family members can restore it too.

How does backup work?

If iCloud is on for Wallworthy (Settings → [your name] → iCloud), your galleries are backed up to your private iCloud automatically and appear on your other devices. A shared gallery is kept in the iCloud of the person who started it and counts toward their storage, at about 1 MB a drawing. If your iCloud storage is full, new art stays on your iPhone until there's room. You can also use Export everything to save a copy that opens on any computer.

How do I share a gallery with my partner or the grandparents?

Open the ••• menu → Share with family → Invite someone, and send the invite by Messages or email. Everyone needs Wallworthy 1.1 or later, so update from the App Store first. When they open it on their iPhone and tap Join, you both see the same children, drawings and voice notes, and either of you can add more. Any drawings they already had are added to the shared gallery. Sharing is free, and each person uses their own Apple Account.

What happens if I stop sharing or leave?

Everyone keeps a copy of every drawing on their own iPhone, backed up to their own iCloud. From then on, new drawings are no longer shared. The person who started the gallery can also remove one person from Share with family → Invite or remove people.

How do I leave kid mode?

Tap the 🔒 Kid mode button at the top of the screen and answer the grown-up question.

How do I print a book?

Open a school year and choose Make the picture book. You can print it with AirPrint, or save the 8 × 8 inch PDF and upload it to any photo-book service.
