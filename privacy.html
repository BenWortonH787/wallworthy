Wallworthy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: Wallworthy does not collect, see, sell or share anything about you or your children. Your family's art stays with your family.

What Wallworthy stores, and where

The artwork you scan, your children's names and pictures, and any voice notes you record are saved on your iPhone.

If iCloud is turned on for Wallworthy, Apple also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud account so it's backed up and appears on your other devices. That copy is protected by your Apple Account. We (the makers of Wallworthy) have no way to access it.

We do not run servers that receive your art, and there is no Wallworthy account to sign up for.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party data collection.
  • No selling or sharing of data, ever.
  • No contact with your children. Kid mode keeps purchases and settings behind a grown-up check.

Camera, microphone and photos

Wallworthy asks for the camera to scan artwork and the microphone to record your child describing their art. It asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save a framed picture there. Choosing photos to import uses Apple's private photo picker, so Wallworthy sees only the photos you choose. All image and sound processing happens on your device.

Sharing

When you send art, a video or a book to family, you choose the app and the recipients (for example Messages or email). What happens after that is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Purchases

Wallworthy Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete any artwork or child in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Wallworthy. Before deleting, you can use Export everything in the app to keep a copy.

Children's privacy

Wallworthy is designed for parents and caregivers. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We will not begin collecting data without asking you first.
